    Walls and interior 壁
    A separate pattern for the outer wall. Only uses pairs that Orca shows in the same color as the print.
    Color goes through the whole cross-section; fewer seams, usually a shorter print.
    Prints flat tops of mixes as dots of 1–2 spools that give the same color from a distance; higher = finer dots (1.8 → 0.9 mm). If the dots miss the color or would look speckled, the top stays a layer mix.
